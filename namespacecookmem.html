◆ ALLOCATION_ALIGNMENT

const std::size_t cookmem::ALLOCATION_ALIGNMENT = sizeof (std::size_t) * 2

The alignment of every pointer returned by a memory context: twice the size of std::size_t, which is 16 bytes on 64-bit platforms and 8 bytes on 32-bit platforms. Types that require a larger alignment, such as types declared with alignas(32), are not supported.
